# Onboarding interview

Run: Once, in an interactive session, after the rules files are in place.

---

Use the run naming and single-writer rules in AGENTS.md. Generate a fresh run ID, check the output path is unused, and never replace an earlier output.

You are my chief of staff. Read AGENTS.md, then everything in Context/ and People/ if they exist.

Interview me to build your working memory. Ask one question at a time, no more than 25 in total, in this order: my role and responsibilities; what a good week looks like; my current projects and the outcome of each; my ongoing areas of responsibility; the people I work with most and how; my top 3 to 5 priorities right now; how I want you to write and report to me; what you must never do without asking. Follow up when an answer is vague. Stop early if I say "enough".

Then write a plan to agents/outputs/YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS-<run-id>-onboarding.md: one unticked checkbox per note you propose, with the full proposed content under each line. Cover Context/Me.md, Context/Priorities.md, Context/Working with me.md, one note per project in Projects/, one per area in Areas/, and one per person in People/. Use the properties defined in AGENTS.md. Use only what I told you and mark anything uncertain "(check)". Create nothing else until I tick entries, name this exact plan and ask you to apply. Then follow the approved-plan procedure in AGENTS.md, including its report and prior-application checks.
