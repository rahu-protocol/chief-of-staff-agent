# Morning brief

Run: Scheduled, weekdays, before you start work.

---

Use the run naming and single-writer rules in AGENTS.md. Generate a fresh run ID, check the output path is unused, and never replace an earlier output.

Write my morning brief.

Check the local date and time first. If it is Saturday or Sunday, or after 11:00, append "morning-brief skipped (late or weekend)" to agents/log.md and stop.

Read Context/Priorities.md, Context/Decisions.md, every note in Projects/ and Tasks/ with status active or waiting, and yesterday's entries in agents/log.md. If a calendar connector is available, read today's events. Never create, change or respond to events.

Write agents/outputs/YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS-<run-id>-morning-brief.md with these sections, at most five bullets each:
- Top three today, each linked to its note, with one line on why it matters today
- Due or overdue
- Waiting on others
- Meetings that need prep, and what prep
- Decisions due for revisit
- One question for me

Link notes with [[wikilinks]]. Write "None" under an empty section. Never invent tasks, dates or meetings. Append one line to agents/log.md.
